# FoodAllo — Fair allocation of food and essentials

**FoodAllo** is a public-interest **open concept** for fair food and essential non-food allocation when markets fail. People report needs; transparent rules decide who gets what — need, not influence.

FoodAllo complements existing aid and public emergency stockpiles. It does not replace them. It answers the missing question: *who gets what when*.

**Open concept, professional implementation.** The concept and public rule walkthrough are openly documented. FoodAllo provides pilot support, implementation, training and operation for municipalities, authorities and relief organisations.

## How it works (short)

1. **Need** — Households report food and essentials needs.
2. **Rules** — Weekly shares by age; infants, children, and older people first when stock is tight; per-household caps stop hoarding.
3. **Channels** — App and web, plus desk, neighbour/proxy, and paper codes for people without phones.
4. **Visibility** — Everyone sees the same picture: what is in stock, what is short, and what each household is entitled to.

## How it complements existing structures

Stockpiles and logistics say what exists and where. Crisis law (for example Germany’s ESVG) provides authority and orderly issue. FoodAllo documents the household-level *who gets what* rule beside those instruments — it does not replace aid pipelines, stockpiles, or law.
