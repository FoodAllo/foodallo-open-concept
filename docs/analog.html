Three ways without a smartphone

Same rules as in the app. No extra share. Only different pickup paths.

One card per household per week. The maximum share applies everywhere — desk, neighbour, paper code.
  1. Desk / counter — You go to a municipal or store counter. Staff checks entitlement and records the maximum share.
  2. Neighbour / proxy — A trusted person picks up for you. Same card — no second quota.
  3. Paper code — You get a short code on paper. One-time use, short validity — then pickup like at the desk.
