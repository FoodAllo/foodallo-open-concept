Principles

Equal worth. Need, not influence. Visible shares.

Fair share, not first come

Per-person weekly baselines; infants, children, and elderly first when stock is tight. No household above a capped slice of remaining stock.

Self-reported, then checked

Needs declared, grants and collections recorded — a digital equivalent of a stamped ration booklet.

Stock you can see

Community stock and issued amounts are visible in the app — the same warehouse picture for everyone.

FAQ

Honest questions — and how FoodAllo answers

Reserves are not the same as who gets what. Caps stop hoarding. Multi-channel access includes people without phones.

Aren’t reserves + ESVG enough?

Stockpiles answer that and where — not who gets what when. ESVG § 11 and AMK item 22 call for orderly distribution; the allocation layer is still missing. That’s the hero’s third tile.

Doesn’t an app exclude people without phones?

Digital majority ≠ digital obligation. FoodAllo uses app + web + desk (municipality/retail), proxy for relatives, paper emergency codes, and offline checkout validation. Multi-channel access is mandatory.

Isn’t this surveillance?

Purpose limitation (crisis/preparedness only), data minimization (entitlement + quota, no lasting baskets), separated roles, deletion windows. No consumer profiles for bystanders.

Won’t hoarding and black markets bypass it?

That’s why caps and vulnerable-first — visible in Open allocation: a 25 kg flour request is capped to baseline; first-come empties the warehouse. Audit trails and checkout matching raise acceptance when fairness is visible.

Too complex, costly, slow?

Staged rollout, not a big bang: paper start → limited digital → situation-picture interface. Public interest, no vendor lock-in.

Does federalism block every system?

AMK item 22 calls for nationwide coordination and a BLE situation picture. FoodAllo is an allocation layer with clear lead — not 16 siloed systems and not a parallel authority.

FoodAllo complements aid, it does not replace it — an allocation layer, not a supply chain.
