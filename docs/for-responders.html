For crisis responders · Rule set ready to review

Aid gets food to the warehouse. Allocation decides who gets it.

FoodAllo is a free, public-interest allocation layer for food and life-essential non-food when markets fail. It complements humanitarian aid, stockpiles, and legal preparedness — it does not replace them.

Who this page is for

Humanitarian responders, preparedness planners, municipalities, and anyone who must answer who gets what when under shortage — without turning crisis allocation into first-come chaos.

What FoodAllo adds

  • Need-based shares: basic weekly amounts, with infants, children and older people first when stock is tight.
  • Household caps so hoarding cannot empty the warehouse.
  • Multi-channel access: app, web, desk, neighbour/proxy, paper emergency codes — same max share without a smartphone.
  • Transparent rules you can walk through before a crisis, including an open elimination engine.

What FoodAllo is not

  • Not a claim to end all hunger or produce food
  • Not a replacement for WFP/aid pipelines, national stockpiles, or crisis law
  • Not standing welfare or consumer surveillance profiling
  • Not a paid product or vendor lock-in pitch

How to apply the concept

Use the public pages as a shared rule reference; they are walkthroughs, not a live distribution service. For operation, stage from paper codes to limited digital to a shared picture of stock and shortages. Keep FoodAllo as the who-gets-what rule next to existing aid and stockpile systems — not a parallel supply chain.
