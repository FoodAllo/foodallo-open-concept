## What FoodAllo is

FoodAllo is a public-interest open concept for fair allocation of food and life-essential non-food when markets fail. Households report needs; published rules decide shares (vulnerable-first, household caps). Multi-channel access includes desk, proxy and paper codes for people without smartphones.

It complements humanitarian aid, public stockpiles and crisis law (e.g. Germany’s ESVG). It does not replace them. It addresses the missing *who gets what when* layer.

## What is published today

- Public landing and rule walkthrough (no account)
- Pages for authorities and crisis responders
- Analog / no-smartphone channel description
- Open documentation in this repository (MIT)

## What is not claimed

- No live nationwide distribution service
- No claim to end all hunger or produce food
- No fabricated customers, contracts, partners or revenue figures

## Professional implementation

The concept is openly documented. FoodAllo offers pilot support, implementation, training and operation for municipalities, authorities and relief organisations.
